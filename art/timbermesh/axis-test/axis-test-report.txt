Interpretation of the two runs
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The official plugin DOES convert Blender Z-up -> game Y-up, and it also
  mirrors X and Z (node_builder writes export = (-bx, bz, -by) and reverses
  triangle winding to compensate).
* On raw ART-01 files the plugin therefore yields X [-1.5, 1.5], Y [0, 1],
  Z [-1, 1]: it keeps the centred pivot of the requirement document.
* The game's block-object local space is NOT centred. Measured from the
  shipped ShantySpeaker.IronTeeth.Model.timbermesh of the official modding
  SDK: a 1 x 1 x 2 decoration occupies X [0, 1], Z [0, 1] and is up to
  Y 1.87 tall; vanilla blueprint CollidersSpec centres agree
  (BuildersHut 3x2 box center (1.5, 0.5, 1.0); ShantySpeaker center
  (0.5, 0.25, 0.5)). So models are authored with the BLOCK CORNER at the
  origin, spanning [0, size] on X and Z, front at Z = 0.
* The exporter therefore mirrors X, shifts by half a footprint and shifts
  the depth by +1.0 so that requirement-13.6 contract coordinates map to
    export = (contract.x + 1.5, contract.y, contract.z + 1.0).
* Verified mapping used by the exporter:
    export = (-blender_out.x, blender_out.z, -blender_out.y)
    blender_out = (-src.x - 1.5, src.y - 1.0, src.z)
  i.e. export = (src.x + 1.5, src.z, -src.y + 1.0) = contract + offsets.
